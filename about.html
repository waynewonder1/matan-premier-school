With a passion for early childhood education, [he/she/they] established Matan Premier Schools to provide a nurturing environment that fosters curiosity, creativity, and confidence in every child.

Who we are

Matan Premier Schools was founded to give young children a place where structure and play work hand in hand. Our classrooms combine hands-on discovery with steady routines, so every child grows in confidence at their own pace.

Our small class sizes mean teachers know every child by name — and every family by name too. From the moment a child walks through our gates to the moment they're picked up, they're known, cared for, and encouraged to be curious.

We're proud of the community we've built in Kiamumbi, and we'd love to welcome your family into it.
