Who We Are

Where structure and play work hand in hand.

Matan Premier Schools was founded to give young children a place where structure and play work hand in hand. Our classrooms combine hands-on discovery with steady routines so every child can grow in confidence at their own pace.

Our small class sizes mean teachers know every child by name — and every family by name too. From the moment a child walks through our gates to the moment they are picked up, they are known, cared for and encouraged to be curious.

We are proud of the community we have built in Kiamumbi, and we would love to welcome your family into it.

Our Founder

Martin Okiyo

Martin Okiyo founded Matan Premier Schools with a vision to create a place where young children can flourish through a thoughtful balance of structured learning and meaningful play.

His vision is rooted in creating an environment where every child feels known, supported and encouraged to develop curiosity, confidence and a genuine love of learning.
