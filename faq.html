Questions & Answers

Everything parents ask us.

Quick answers about ages, school hours, fees, meals and transport at Matan Premier Kindergarten.

About the school

What ages do you take?

We welcome children from 2 to 5 years old, from daycare and playgroup through to pre-primary (PP1 and PP2).

Is Matan a new school?

Yes — Matan Premier Kindergarten opened its doors in 2026. Our classes are small, so every child gets plenty of attention and every family is known by name.

How do children learn at Matan?

Through a play-based approach with gentle structure. Daily routines, hands-on activities and outdoor play build early literacy, numeracy, confidence and independence.

Where are you located?

Kiamumbi, Quito Street · QS 54, off Kamiti Road. You are welcome to book a visit and see the school for yourself.

The school day

What are the school hours?

The school day runs from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Every child gets a well-balanced breakfast and lunch, plus a small snack before they go home. Meals are included in the school fees.

My child has an allergy or special diet. Can you help?

Please tell us on the application form or when you visit. We will talk with you about your child's needs before they start.

Fees & transport

How much are the school fees?

School fees are about KES 20,000 per term, and this includes meals. For an exact fee breakdown, please contact us.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes. School transport is available for about KES 7,000 per term. Contact us to check whether we cover your area.

Admissions

How do I enrol my child?

Fill in our short online application form. We will receive it straight away and contact you to arrange a visit and the next steps.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Of course — we would love to meet you. Book a visit or call us on 0712 000 805.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help.
