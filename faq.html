Questions & Answers

Everything parents ask us.

Quick answers about ages, school hours, fees, meals and transport at Matan Premier Kindergarten.

About the school

What ages do you take?

We welcome children from 1 to 6 years old: Daycare (1–2 years), Reception (2–3 years), PP1 (4–5 years) and PP2 (5–6 years).

Is Matan a new school?

Yes — Matan Premier Kindergarten opened its doors in 2026. Our classes are small, so every child gets plenty of attention and every family is known by name.

How do children learn at Matan?

Through a play-based approach with gentle structure. Daily routines, hands-on activities and outdoor play build early literacy, numeracy, confidence and independence.

Where are you located?

Kiamumbi, Quito Street · QS 54, off Kamiti Road. You are welcome to book a visit and see the school for yourself.

The school day

What are the school hours?

The school day runs from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

When does the next term start?

Term 1 of 2027 begins on Wednesday, 6th January 2027. Places are open now — you can apply online.

What activities do children take part in?

Alongside classroom learning, children enjoy swimming, outdoor play, our mini-shamba gardening project and practical life activities. In 2027 every learner takes at least one enrichment activity — swimming, music, ballet / creative dance or skating (KES 4,000 per term each) — and we are building a bigger playground.

Do you support children with additional needs?

Yes. We work with occupational therapy services to support learners with additional developmental and learning needs. Please tell us about your child's needs on the application form or when you visit.

Are meals provided?

Yes. Meals are included in tuition, following the school meal plan.

My child has an allergy or special diet. Can you help?

Please tell us on the application form or when you visit. We will talk with you about your child's needs before they start.

Fees & transport

How much are the school fees?

Tuition for 2027 is KES 20,000 per term for Daycare (or KES 400 per day), KES 34,000 for Reception, KES 35,000 for PP1 and KES 36,000 for PP2. Tuition includes meals, stationery and core learning clubs. See the full Fees & Transport page, including annual and admission fees.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes. Transport for morning pick-up and afternoon drop-off is charged per term by zone, from KES 8,000 (Kiamumbi Estate) to KES 25,000. One way is 75% of the zone fee. See the transport zones to find your area.

Admissions

How do I enrol my child?

Fill in our short online application form. There is a one-time admission fee of KES 6,000. We will receive it straight away and contact you to arrange a visit and the next steps.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Of course — we would love to meet you. Book a visit or call us on 0712 000 805.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help.
