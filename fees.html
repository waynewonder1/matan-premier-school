Transport terms

  • Fees are charged on a termly basis.
  • One way only is charged at 75% of the zone fee.
  • Transport is subject to route availability and vehicle capacity.
  • Pick-up and drop-off locations and times will be agreed with parents.
  • Extended routes or special arrangements are agreed with the school administration.
  • Final zone placement may change after confirming actual van routes and distance.
